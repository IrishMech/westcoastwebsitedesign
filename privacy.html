Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

West Coast Website Design respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains the limited ways information may be handled when you visit westcoastwebsitedesign.com or communicate with West Coast Website Design.

Information You Provide

This website does not currently provide contact forms, customer accounts, payment forms, or other features that ask visitors to submit personal information directly through the website.

If you choose to contact West Coast Website Design by email, the information contained in your email, including your email address and any information you voluntarily provide, will be received and used for the purpose of responding to your inquiry and communicating with you about services you request.

Please do not send sensitive personal information, financial account information, medical information, passwords, Social Security numbers, or other highly confidential information by email.

Information Associated With Website Hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Like many website hosting services, the hosting provider may automatically process technical information associated with visits to the site.

GitHub states that visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes when GitHub Pages websites are visited.

Information processed by GitHub is subject to GitHub's own privacy and security practices. West Coast Website Design does not control the technical logging performed by GitHub's hosting infrastructure.

Cookies and Tracking

West Coast Website Design does not currently use its own advertising pixels, behavioral advertising trackers, or website analytics services.

The website may rely on third-party infrastructure, including hosting and web-font services, which may process limited technical information necessary to provide their services.

How Information Is Used

Information voluntarily provided through direct communication may be used to:

  • Respond to questions or service inquiries.
  • Discuss potential website design or related services.
  • Provide requested estimates, proposals, or business communications.
  • Maintain records reasonably necessary for business operations.

Selling Personal Information

West Coast Website Design does not sell personal information provided through direct business communications.

Data Retention

Business communications may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to an inquiry, provide requested services, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, or satisfy legal obligations.

Information that is no longer reasonably necessary may be deleted as part of normal business record management.

Third-Party Services

This website uses GitHub Pages for website hosting and Google-hosted font resources for website typography.

Those companies operate under their own privacy policies and may process technical information independently of West Coast Website Design.

External Links

This website may contain links to websites or services operated by third parties. West Coast Website Design is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party websites.

Children's Privacy

This website and its services are intended for businesses and adults seeking website-related services. West Coast Website Design does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through this website.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, services, or privacy practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
